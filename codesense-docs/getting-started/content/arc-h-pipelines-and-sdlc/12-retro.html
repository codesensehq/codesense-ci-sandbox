What this phase is for

A retro's job is to turn evidence from the last sprint into owned actions. bmad-retrospective is deliberately structural — it collects what shipped, verifies the findings against the real artifacts (PRs, CI runs, inbox threads), and requires every proposed change to name an owner.

This is the phase where the retro of one epic drops a finding into the planning of the next: the kill-ladder's grace-period bug surfaced here and seeded the next-sprint DEMO-4 fix.

Local vs remote

Local

Mary reads the shipped artifacts from your worktree + inbox history. The output lands in _bmad-output/<epic>/retro.md for easy diff review.

Remote (private-account)

Remote retros read remote artifacts. The retro agent publishes the retro doc with reverse_sync_worktree so the local epic dashboard shows the carry-over actions.
