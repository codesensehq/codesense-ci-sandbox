What this phase is for

A sprint plan says what the team is doing this cycle. CodeSense keeps it in two places: the team's sprint registry, config/sprints.yaml (each sprint's state, goal, start, end and, once closed, delivered_points and carried_points), and a sprint: field in each committed story's frontmatter. A planning agent, John here, running bmad-sprint-planning, reads the story graph and past velocity, proposes a commit list, and writes those two places once you accept it. You can edit them by hand too, or right-click a story in the left nav to Add to or Move to a sprint.

The sprint-analysis.html example dashboard reads the registry and the stories live: velocity from closed sprints, and done versus committed points for the active one. A story's progress (todo → in-progress → review → done) comes straight from its status field. CodeSense doesn't run a sprint for you: agents pick up stories when you or a routine starts them.

Local vs remote

Local

Sprint planning is interactive and writes config/sprints.yaml and the stories' sprint: fields. Dashboards that read them update live.

Remote (private-account)

Same shape. An agent on a machine sees the stories hydrated into its workspace; to see your latest, it calls hydrate_worktree. Edits it makes there come back to your context only through reverse_sync_worktree (preview first with preview_reverse_sync_worktree).
