What this phase is for

You've got a nagging itch — something in your repo is awkward, a teammate keeps asking for the same thing, or you watched the agent DAG grind through a bad path for the third time. The Idea phase names it: a sentence or two sharp enough that the next agent can start a spec without rephrasing the problem.

Mary (the analyst persona) is the default owner. She leans on the bmad-brainstorming and bmad-forge-idea skills — the first for divergent exploration, the second to hammer the idea until it has a shape or dies cheaply.

Local vs remote

Local

Running Mary on your own Mac is the common case. She reads your context repo straight off disk and writes drafts into content/. No special setup — the host agent is already there.

Remote (private-account)

On a remote macbook, Mary runs under the private account's host agent. Her ideas reach team content when she publishes them with reverse_sync_worktree; nothing comes back on its own. Same skill, same inbox role.
